[0:00 – 0:18] What is MockNiner & Why It Exists
MockNiner is a universal exam engine that runs entirely inside your browser. It’s 100% local—no backend servers, no application API keys, and no need to buy another expensive SaaS subscription.

Students already pay for AI agents like ChatGPT. MockNiner connects those agents directly to a real exam environment through WebMCP, turning your existing agent into your personal test creator and tutor.

[0:18 – 0:49] IELTS Listening: Agent Authoring & Local Execution
Here, I ask the agent to make me a full forty-question IELTS Listening test.

Through WebMCP tools, the agent authors the questions and dialogue scripts, and installs the test right into MockNiner. The application generates the speech audio locally on-device, manages the 30-minute timer, and tracks navigation.

I listen, answer questions in the browser, and submit. The app scores my answers locally and saves my attempt for review.

[0:49 – 1:38] IELTS Writing: Two-Way Collaboration & Real Feedback
For subjective practice like IELTS Writing, MockNiner and the agent collaborate seamlessly.

I ask for practice, and the agent opens an academic test with an authentic data chart and timer. I write my responses and submit. The app creates an immutable submission with a unique attempt ID.

Now, I ask my agent to grade it. Using WebMCP, the agent reads my submitted text, evaluates both tasks against official IELTS criteria, and attaches feedback directly back to my saved attempt.

The results appear right on the page—including band scores, criterion breakdowns, and side-by-side inline corrections showing exactly how to improve.

[1:38 – 2:07] SAT Practice: A Universal Exam Engine
MockNiner is built as a universal engine, not just a single exam app.

Here, I ask for an SAT practice test. The agent authors complete Reading, Writing, and Math modules, and WebMCP opens the SAT interface with official section timing and question navigation.

I work through the module, choose my answers, and submit. The app calculates my accuracy and breaks down my performance by section and skill topic.

[2:07 – 2:35] Custom Exams (NEET) & The WebMCP Vision
And you’re never limited to built-in exams. Here, I ask for a custom thirty-question NEET practice set covering Biology, Physics, Chemistry, and Math.

The agent authors the custom assessment on the fly, and MockNiner instantly renders it into a fully interactive test.

This is the true power of WebMCP: the agent supplies the intelligence, while MockNiner provides the local, private exam experience—without any backend servers, extra subscriptions, or data leaving your device.

MockNiner brings AI into the real learning workflow: from creating practice to mastering the exam.
